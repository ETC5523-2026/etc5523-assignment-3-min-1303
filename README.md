## About This Website

This personal website was created for ETC5523: Communicating with Data at Monash University. It introduces my background in Business Analytics and explores how complex research findings can be communicated through accessible writing and effective data visualisation.

The featured blog post, *Could Vaccines Keep Border Quarantine Safe Against Delta?*, adapts my Assignment 2 technical report based on the research of Zachreson et al. (2022).

## Intended Audience

The blog post is intended for non-technical Australian readers interested in understanding how vaccination and emerging COVID-19 variants affected the effectiveness of border quarantine.
